**Section line:** "We are well-equipped for a variety of event settings"

**Add-ons, upon request:**

- DJ or live band — music to suit the mood
- Stage & décor — themed styling and a photo booth
- Full branding — backdrops, banners and a selfie wall

**Dietary needs, well looked after**
> Worried about restrictions? Our kitchen team is well-trained to handle a wide range of needs, from allergies and intolerances to food preferences. You will be in good hands.

Diets catered for: Vegetarian, Seafood/shellfish-free, No pork, Gluten-free, Dairy-free/lactose-free, Nut-free/nut allergy.

How guests are kept safe:
1. **Tell us early** — Share any restrictions or allergies when you book, and we'll plan around every guest.
2. **Separated prep** — Special meals are prepared in a separate area of the kitchen to reduce the risk of cross-contact.
3. **Named plates** — Each special meal is labelled with the diner's name, so it always reaches the right guest.
4. **Documented recipes** — Ingredients and allergens for every dish are recorded, so we can answer any question.

## 6. Booking

**Section line:** "Host your special event in 5 simple steps"

1. **Enquire** — Share your occasion, date, guest numbers and budget.
2. **Review** — We send you a tailored package within 1–2 days.
3. **Plan** — Your event planner locks in the menu, layout, timings and headcount.
4. **Confirm** — Approve the package and pay the deposit to book your date.
5. **Enjoy** — On the day, we take care of everything so you can relax with your guests.

**Good to know**

*Cancellation:*
- 15+ days: full refund
- 8–14 days: 25% of estimated total
- 7 days or less: deposit not refunded

*Payment:*
- Deposit: 50% of the estimated total
- Balance: within 2 weeks after your event
- Method: ABA transfer or card (credit/debit)

*Changes:*
- Guest numbers, menu or set-up: we appreciate 3 working days' notice before your event.
- "Detailed terms & conditions are provided in your proposal."
